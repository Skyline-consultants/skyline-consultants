UK Study Visa Information

Important information for students

Visa rules can change. The information below is for general website guidance and should be checked against the official UK government pages before an application.

Student visa application fee

£558
Current figure shown on GOV.UK. Check the official page before applying.

Monthly funds in London

£1,529
Maintenance amount per month where the financial requirement applies.

Monthly funds outside London

£1,171
Maintenance amount per month where the financial requirement applies.

Student visa basics

  • You normally need an offer from a licensed student sponsor.
  • You normally need a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS).
  • Financial evidence may be required depending on your circumstances.
  • Academic progression rules can apply to students extending from inside the UK.
  • Application timing and eligibility depend on your individual circumstances.
Official GOV.UK Student visa information →

Graduate visa

  • Applications made on or before 31 December 2026 are shown by GOV.UK as having a 2 year duration.
  • Applications made from 1 January 2027 are shown as having an 18 month duration.
  • PhD or doctoral graduates can have a 3 year Graduate visa.
  • The Graduate visa cannot itself be extended.
Official GOV.UK Graduate visa information →
Important: Skyline Consultants provides study and application guidance through this website. Immigration rules are subject to change and individual eligibility varies. Students should check GOV.UK and obtain regulated immigration advice where appropriate.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can Skyline help students who are already in the UK?

Yes. The website is designed for students already in the UK as well as students outside the UK who are exploring further study and UK university options.

Can I study another Master's after completing a Master's?

Possibility and visa eligibility depend on the student's circumstances, previous study, proposed course and current immigration rules. Students should have their individual situation checked before applying.

Can I use the Course Finder as a final course list?

No. It is an initial exploration tool. Course availability, fees, entry requirements, English requirements, intakes and deadlines can change and should be verified on the university's official website.

Does the website guarantee a visa?

No. A university offer or consultancy guidance does not guarantee a visa. Visa decisions are made under the applicable UK immigration rules.

How do I contact Skyline?

You can use the consultation form, the WhatsApp buttons or the phone number shown on the website.
